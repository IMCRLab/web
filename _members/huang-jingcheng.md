Jingcheng Huang is a PhD Candidate in the School of Mechanical Science and Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. The research covers embodied intelligence and human–robot interaction, with a focus on facial-expression generation for humanoid robots and on building interaction systems. The work studies coordinated generation of facial and body motion, aiming to make robot interaction more humanlike and to support natural, fluent affective communication.
