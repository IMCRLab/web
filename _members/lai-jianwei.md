Jianwei Lai is a 2023 undergraduate in mechanical design, manufacturing, and automation at the School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, from Ganzhou, Jiangxi, and a member of the interaction group. During undergraduate study he competed in VEX and RoboCup as a core mechanical member, responsible for mechanism design and full-system modeling, and received several national awards. His research interest is robot mechanism design and interaction.
