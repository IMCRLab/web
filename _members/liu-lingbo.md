Lingbo Liu is a second-year Master Student in the School of Mechanical Science and Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. The research covers dexterous manipulation and multimodal perception, with a focus on force–tactile representation learning for hand–object interaction, and on building manipulation datasets and task policies. The aim is to give robots a more intuitive ability to complete physical manipulation tasks on their own.
