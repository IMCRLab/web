Fuxiang Fan studies flexible magnetic tactile sensors. Using an Ecoflex–NdFeB composite, the work fabricates soft magnetoelastic films, investigates how mechanical loads map to magnetic signals, and characterizes sensor performance, aiming to provide a new soft tactile sensing approach for humanoid robots and human–robot interaction.
{% endcapture %}

主要研究磁柔性触觉传感器。基于Ecoflex‑钕铁硼复合体系制备柔性磁弹性薄膜，探究力学载荷与磁信号输出之间的响应规律，并完成传感器件的系统性能表征，旨在为人形机器人等场景下的人机交互领域提供新型柔性触觉感知方案。
