Minshi Liang is a Master Student who received a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. The research covers flexible magnetic tactile sensing and multimodal touch, including magnetic-film fabrication, sensor structure design, and three-axis force decoupling, and explores applications in robotic electronic skin.
