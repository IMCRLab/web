Sheng Li is a Master Student in the 2025 cohort at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. The work covers structural design of humanoid robotic faces and speech-driven lip motion. On the design side, the focus is mechanism optimization that balances biomimetic accuracy and engineering feasibility. On motion generation, the focus is speech-driven lip deformation and viseme synchronization, so that interaction feels more natural and approachable.

{% capture zh %}
李胜，华中科技大学 25 级硕士研究生，主要从事仿人面部机器人的结构设计与仿人语音唇部动作生成研究。在结构设计方面，聚焦面部机构的构型优化建模，力求兼顾仿生精度与工程可行性；在动作生成方面，探索由语音驱动唇部形变与口型同步的方法，以提升人机交互的自然度与亲和力。
