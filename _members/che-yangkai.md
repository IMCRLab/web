{% capture en %}
Yangkai Che studies electromagnetic nondestructive testing and physics-informed neural networks. The work addresses the ill-posed inverse problem of reconstructing a magnetic field from sparse observations under bias and disturbance. It combines electromagnetic constraints with measured data to localize metal defects and recover their shape for inspection in complex working conditions.
{% endcapture %}

主要研究方向为电磁无损检测与物理信息神经网络，针对偏置扰动下稀疏磁场观测带来的逆问题病态性，开展机理-数据协同的磁场鲁棒重构与金属缺陷定量反演研究。以电磁场物理约束为基础，构建物理相容的反演框架，提升缺陷定位与形貌重建精度，为复杂工况下智能电磁检测提供方法支撑。
