Yaxuan Li studies electromagnetic nondestructive testing and physics-informed neural networks, inverting metal defects from sparse magnetic-field observations. The work combines electromagnetic simulation, physics-constrained modeling, and data-driven methods for defect localization and geometry reconstruction, with an interest in engineering applications of intelligent sensing and inspection.
