Xinrong Zhang is a Master Student in mechanical engineering in the 2026 cohort at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. The research is robot limb-motion generation for human–robot interaction. Current work combines multimodal perception and coordinated motion on humanoid robots, exploring how interaction signals can produce stable, natural limb movements that fit human habits.
